# Slideshow Prompt — Harvest Harmonics 3-Tier Automation Demo

Use this prompt with a slide-generation tool (Gamma, Beautiful.ai, ChatGPT
w/ slides, Canva Magic Design, etc.). 5 slides, one per section below.

---

## Prompt to paste in

```
Create a 5-slide presentation titled "Harvest Harmonics: 3-Tier Automation Demo."
Style: clean, modern, dark-green/earth-tone palette (farming/agtech feel —
avoid generic corporate blue), minimal text per slide, icon-forward, one clear
takeaway per slide. Audience: a small agtech company's hiring team evaluating
a free unsolicited automation demo. Tone: confident but not salesy — this is a
gift/credibility piece, not a pitch deck.

Slide 1 — Intro
Title: "I built this for you, for free"
Subtitle: "3 working automations scoped to Harvest Harmonics — built in n8n,
transferable to GoHighLevel"
Content: Brief line that this was built after seeing the "Go High Level Expert
& AI Engineer" job posting, using Harvest Harmonics' own public pricing page
and real operational scale (100+ US farms, 2-year YieldMAX cycles) as the
source material. No sales pitch — everything is public and free regardless
of outcome. Include a small 3-icon preview teaser (gear / chat bubble /
dashboard) foreshadowing the next 3 slides.

Slide 2 — Tier 1: Lead & Pricing Router (simple automation, no AI)
Title: "Every lead, instantly matched to the right price"
Problem: Inbound leads require a rep to manually look up which pricing tier
applies (flat starter rate, per-acre small grower, YieldMAX 10-29 acres,
volume discount 30+, or pivot program $10k/$20k) before replying.
Solution: A deterministic rules engine reads acreage + irrigation type and
auto-routes to the correct quote or flags for a sales consult — zero AI,
zero hallucination risk, instant.
Why this tier: Cheap, fast, no ongoing AI cost — don't reach for AI when a
lookup table solves it.

Slide 3 — Tier 2: Farmer Inquiry Triage (n8n + AI)
Title: "Messy questions in, grounded answers out"
Problem: Inbound messages are a mix of skeptics, organic-certification
questions, urgent post-install technical issues, and plain price requests —
someone has to read and triage every one by hand.
Solution: AI classifies each inquiry, flags anything urgent (active
installation issues, time-sensitive irrigation events) for immediate staff
alert, and drafts a reply grounded only in real Harvest Harmonics facts (32
university trials + 430 underway, 15-30% documented yield increase) for a
human to approve before sending.
Why this tier: AI earns its keep here — judgment and drafting from
unstructured text, always human-reviewed before going out.

Slide 4 — Tier 3: Farm Renewal Tracker (local dashboard app)
Title: "100+ farms, one view, nothing falls through"
Problem: At 100+ active US farms on 2-year YieldMAX renewal cycles, "who
renews when" and "who hasn't been contacted in months" outgrows a
spreadsheet or basic CRM pipeline view.
Solution: A live dashboard with independent traffic-light flags for renewal
proximity and contact recency across every farm, plus one-click AI-drafted
renewal, check-in, or win-back messages — each grounded in that specific
farm's real documented results, never fabricated.
Why this tier: Only justified once volume/coordination genuinely outgrows
simpler tools — this is the "don't over-build" lesson in action.

Slide 5 — Conclusion
Title: "All public, all free, all yours either way"
Content: Link to the GitHub repo (public, importable, free to use regardless
of hiring decision). One line restating the n8n-to-GHL transferability:
"every idea here — the pricing logic, the triage prompts, the renewal data
model — maps 1:1 onto GHL workflows, automations, and AI employees." Closing
line: open to building this for real inside their GHL account if the role is
still open, but the tools stand on their own either way.
Include: GitHub repo URL as the single largest element on the slide.

Keep all 5 slides visually consistent (same color palette, same icon style,
same title font treatment). No stock-photo filler — use simple icons/shapes
only.
```
